About Harvard University

Founded in 1636, Harvard University is the oldest institution of higher education in the United States. Located in Cambridge, Massachusetts, Harvard is internationally recognized for its excellence in teaching, research, innovation, and leadership development. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs across diverse fields, supported by distinguished faculty, world-class libraries, and cutting-edge research facilities.

Vision

To advance knowledge, foster innovation, and educate future leaders who will make meaningful contributions to society through excellence in teaching, learning, and research.

Mission

Harvard University's mission is to educate students to become responsible citizens and leaders, promote academic excellence, encourage critical thinking and creativity, and advance knowledge through groundbreaking research that benefits communities around the world.

Eligibility
  • Completion of secondary (high) school or equivalent qualification.
  • Applying for your first bachelor's degree (students who already hold a bachelor's degree are not eligible for Harvard College undergraduate admission).
  • Strong academic performance in rigorous coursework.
  • Submission of required standardized test scores (SAT or ACT), where required by the current admissions policy.
  • International and U.S. applicants are considered under the same admissions process
Admission Steps
  • Complete the Common Application.
  • Answer the Harvard College supplemental questions.
  • Pay the application fee (or request a fee waiver if eligible).
  • Submit official school reports, transcripts, and teacher recommendations.
  • Submit SAT/ACT scores if required under the current admissions policy.
  • Track your application through the Harvard Applicant Portal after receiving your confirmation email.
  • Receive your admission decision and, if admitted, complete enrollment and financial aid requirements.
Required Documents
  • Completed Common Application
  • Harvard supplemental questions
  • High school transcript
  • School Report and counselor recommendation
  • Two teacher recommendation letters
  • Personal essay
  • Activities/extracurricular list
  • Midyear School Report (when applicable)
  • SAT or ACT scores (per current admissions policy)
  • Optional AP, IB, A-Level, or other examination results
  • English proficiency documentation if applicable for international applicants.
